## What the App does

The App posts short AI-written summaries (“TL;DRs”) of busy comment threads as
moderator comments, can write a weekly digest of a community’s top threads, and
gives moderators tools to preview, edit, post, or skip summaries. If a
community’s moderators turn it on, the App also sends popular posts, summaries,
the digest, and report alerts to that community’s Discord server.
